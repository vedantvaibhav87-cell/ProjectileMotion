02 / THE PHYSICS

From equations to motion

01

Resolve velocity

Initial velocity is split into horizontal and vertical components using the launch angle.

vₓ = v₀ cos θ
vᵧ = v₀ sin θ
02

Calculate drag

Quadratic drag depends on air density, drag coefficient, frontal area and relative air speed.

D = ½ ρ Cd A v²
03

Integrate motion

Newton's second law is integrated step-by-step using fourth-order Runge–Kutta (RK4).

F = m a
Δt = 0.01 s
ⓘ

Model assumptions: 2D motion, flat ground, constant gravity, constant air density and constant Cd during each run. Spin, lift, changing atmospheric conditions and altitude-dependent wind are not modelled.
